Line-following robot

Designed and built a robot capable of moving in 360 degrees, programmed to travel specified routes using optical and sonic sensor data to follow paths and avoid collisions.

What it does

Follows pre-programmed paths using black lines on the ground, using optical sensors to track the line and sonic sensors to detect and avoid obstacles in its path.

Tools

Python Raspberry Pi Optical sensors Sonic sensors
